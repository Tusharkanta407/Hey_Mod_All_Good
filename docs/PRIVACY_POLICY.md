# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Devvit application **"Hey mod, all good"** ("App") handles information.

## 1. Who we are

Hey mod, all good is a moderation-assistant app for Reddit communities. The App is intended for use by subreddit moderators.

## 2. Information we process

To provide moderation features, the App may process:

- Reddit post content and metadata (title, body, post ID, subreddit, timestamps)
- Modmail conversation content and metadata (conversation ID, message text)
- Moderation events and actions (reports, automod filter events, approve/remove/archive actions)
- App configuration values set by moderators (for example: operating mode, custom keywords, subreddit rules text)

The App may also use a configured third-party AI service key (`geminiApiKey`) to generate safety summaries for moderation workflows.

## 3. How we use information

We use information only to:

- Detect and prioritize potentially harmful or policy-violating content
- Show protected summaries and blurred previews to moderators
- Enable moderator decisions (approve/remove/archive) in workflow tools
- Generate aggregate queue and emotional-load metrics
- Operate, debug, and secure the App

## 4. Storage and retention

The App stores operational moderation data in Devvit Redis for app functionality (queue state, summaries, and related moderation metadata).

Retention is limited to what is reasonably required to operate moderation workflows. Data may be overwritten or deleted as queue items are handled and rotated.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be shared only:

- With Reddit platform services needed to run the App
- With configured AI provider endpoints required to generate moderation summaries
- When required by law or platform policy

## 6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including restricted moderator access and secret configuration fields for API keys. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is intended for moderator use in accordance with Reddit policies.

## 8. Your choices

Subreddit moderators control whether to install, configure, and use this App. Moderators can remove the App from a subreddit at any time.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted at the policy URL.

## 10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the app developer through the Reddit app page feedback/contact channel.
